How We Work

The 5-Step Client Onboarding Workflow

From first tech pack to a container at port — a fixed, predictable path.

  1. 01

    Tech-Pack Evaluation & Cost Estimation

    We audit your spec sheet, materials, and construction to confirm feasibility and give you a transparent cost breakdown.

  2. 02

    Pattern Drafting & Sample Development

    Patterns are drafted and a physical fit-sample is cut and stitched — delivered in 10–14 days.

  3. 03

    Fit Approval & Pre-Production Sample Sign-Off

    You review the prototype, request revisions, and sign off on the Pre-Production Sample before bulk begins.

  4. 04

    Bulk Cutting, Stitching & Inline Quality Inspection

    Production runs with inline quality checkpoints at cutting, stitching, and finishing — not just a final inspection.

  5. 05

    Global Export Packaging & Door-to-Door Freight Shipping

    Goods are packed for ocean or air freight with door-to-door or port-to-port shipping options.
